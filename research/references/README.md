# Research References

This directory contains the sources consulted during the development of the research.

The references include official technical documentation, professional cybersecurity certification materials, practical cybersecurity training resources, and other relevant sources used to support the research methodology, laboratory implementation, analysis, and discussion.

## Source Categories

### Official Technical Documentation

* Snort official documentation
* pfSense official documentation
* Microsoft technical documentation where applicable

Official documentation is used primarily to support the installation, configuration, operation, and technical understanding of the security technologies implemented in the laboratory.

### Professional Cybersecurity Resources

* EC-Council Certified Ethical Hacker (CEH), Version 13
* CompTIA Security+ official study and examination materials

These resources provide supporting cybersecurity concepts, methodologies, terminology, and practical security-testing principles relevant to the research.

### Practical Cybersecurity Training

* Hack The Box

Hack The Box is used as a practical cybersecurity learning resource to support understanding of security-testing techniques and methodologies.

### Researcher's Professional Knowledge

The research design and practical implementation also draw upon the researcher's professional experience in IT infrastructure, networking, systems engineering, and cybersecurity.

Professional experience informs the design and implementation of the laboratory; however, experimental findings and conclusions are based on evidence collected from the controlled research environment.

## Reference Management

The bibliographic details, documentation URLs, editions, publication information, and access dates for sources used in the research will be recorded and verified as the research progresses.

Sources will be cited where they directly support statements, methodologies, technical configurations, or other material derived from external documentation.
